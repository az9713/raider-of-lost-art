SCENE CONTEXT
A moment after he plunged into the waterfall. The young explorer bursts up through the white water into the churning pool at the foot of the waterfall, gasping, soaked, with no hat and no satchel, the small gold idol thrust high above the water in his right hand. He is swept into the rapids and swims hard with one arm, downstream. He looks for help. The river is empty: no boat, no one. Ahead, the river drops away over a dark lip into mist. Eight seconds, ONE continuous take with no cuts, no dialogue.

LOCATION MAP
The plunge pool at the foot of the huge waterfall from Image 3: the white curtain of the waterfall thundering behind and above, mist rolling off it, steep green jungle walls on both sides. From the pool the river runs away from the waterfall as white-water rapids, bending slightly, and further along it drops away over a dark lip into mist. The river is empty: no boat, no people, no objects. All the water is one continuous body of water, same colour, same foam, same scale from the pool to the rapids to the lip.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Low water-level shot, the waterfall roaring behind him. The surface of the pool is churning white. His head and the idol have not broken the surface yet.

FORMAT MODE
ONE continuous take, no cuts, no edits, no jump, no change of angle. A single unbroken camera move. First part, about 3 seconds: the camera sits at water level in the churning pool, facing the waterfall behind him. He bursts up through the surface, gasping, spluttering water, the idol raised high in his right hand. The current grabs him. Second part, about 5 seconds, the same take: the camera swings around and rises above the water as it follows him downstream, turning to face DOWNSTREAM, the same direction he swims. He swims hard with his left arm and his legs, the idol held high in his right hand, glancing from side to side for help. The camera rises to a wide, high view down the empty river: the rapids ahead of him and, far in front, the dark lip where the river drops into mist. The take ends on that wide view, him small in the foreground water, still swimming toward the drop.

PERFORMANCE
Shock, then fierce effort. He gasps, coughs, shakes water out of his eyes. His face shows desperation as he looks left and right and finds no boat, then dread as he sees the drop ahead. One arm stays raised with the idol whatever the current does. No speaking.

PHYSICS
The current pulls him down the river faster than he swims. White water breaks over his shoulders, he sinks to the chin and surfaces again. Water streams off the idol and off his raised arm. His jacket billows and clings. Spray and mist drift across the frame. The idol is held clear of the water at all times.

LIGHTING
Cool teal-green overcast daylight, soft, no hard sun. Pale foam, deep green jungle, grey-green water. No warm light except the natural reflection on the wet gold.

AUDIO
The waterfall roar behind him, then fading and mixing with the rushing of the rapids. His gasping breath, splashing, water streaming. A deeper, growing rumble ahead, the drop. No engine, no speech, no music.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action adventure thriller, 16:9 anamorphic look, fine film grain, muted green and teal palette. Same look and colour grade as the previous shots.

No glow, no cracks, no light effects on the idol. His face matches Image 1. The river is EMPTY: no boat and no people anywhere in the frame at any point.
