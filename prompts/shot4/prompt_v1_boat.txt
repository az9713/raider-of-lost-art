SCENE CONTEXT
A moment after shot 3. The young explorer is alone in the white-water rapids, swimming hard downstream, soaked, with no hat and no satchel, the small gold idol held high in his right hand. Far ahead the river drops away over a dark lip into mist, and he is being swept toward it. At the last second a small riverboat bursts in from the side, and a hand reaches down from it and pulls him aboard just before the drop. Six seconds, ONE continuous take with no cuts, no dialogue.

LOCATION MAP
The same river as shot 3: grey-green white-water rapids between steep green jungle walls, mist rolling in, and far ahead a dark lip where the whole river drops away into mist. The waterfall is far behind the camera and is not in frame. The drop is ahead of him, in the direction he swims. The boat enters from the LEFT side of the frame, from the jungle bank, and everything stays on this one stretch of river.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
A wide, high view down the river, looking DOWNSTREAM. The explorer is small in the foreground water, swimming away from the camera toward the drop, the idol held high in his right hand. The river ahead is empty. The dark lip of the drop and the mist beyond it fill the far end of the frame.

FORMAT MODE
ONE continuous take, no cuts, no edits, no jump. One fast unbroken camera move. About 1.5 seconds: the camera glides down toward the water, holding the wide view, him swimming toward the lip, the roar of the drop growing. About 1 second: from the left edge of the frame a boat bursts out of the spray at speed, engine roaring, throwing a wall of white water, and the camera whips left to follow it. About 2 seconds: the boat slews alongside him, close, at water level, the camera tight on him and the hull. About 1.5 seconds: a hand reaches down from the boat, grabs his left forearm, and hauls him up over the side; the take ends as he lands on the boat floor, the boat turning hard away from the lip, the dark edge of the drop sliding past behind the stern.

PERFORMANCE
He is spent and desperate, still kicking, eyes on the drop, and then he hears the engine and turns his head toward it. Relief hits his face for a moment. He keeps the idol held up and clear in his right hand and reaches with his LEFT arm. The helper is only a hand and a forearm: a weathered brown hand in a faded olive sleeve, gripping hard. The helper's face is NEVER shown: the pilot stays behind the console, out of frame, seen at most as a dark shape from behind, with no face at any moment. No speaking.

PHYSICS
The current drags him toward the lip faster than he swims. The boat's wake slams over him. Water streams off the idol, his raised arm and his jacket. When the hand grips him his left arm snaps taut, his body drags through the water, then he is lifted over the gunwale and falls onto the wet floor of the boat. The boat heels over as it turns. The idol is held clear of the water, and then clear of the hull, at all times; he never lets go of it.

LIGHTING
Cool teal-green overcast daylight, soft, no hard sun. Pale foam, deep green jungle, grey-green water, white mist. No warm light except the natural reflection on the wet gold.

AUDIO
The growing rumble of the drop ahead, rushing water, his gasping breath. Then a sudden outboard engine roar from the left, a crash of spray, a hard thud of the hull, a grunt as he is hauled up. No speech, no music.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action adventure thriller, 16:9 anamorphic look, fine film grain, muted green and teal palette. Same look and colour grade as the previous shots.
