SCENE CONTEXT
Inside an ancient, overgrown jungle temple chamber. Cool teal-green daylight falls through a collapsed roof. A young explorer lifts a small polished-gold idol off a stone altar. The temple reacts: dust falls and the floor shakes. He looks up, alarmed. Eight seconds, two cuts, no dialogue.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Wide-medium shot. The explorer stands at the altar, front three-quarter view, both hands reaching toward the idol. The idol sits on the altar top. The light shaft is behind him.

FORMAT MODE
Sequence of two cuts, no timecodes. The camera does not cut on its own, only at the cut points below.
CUT 1, medium shot, slow push-in, about 5 seconds: the explorer lifts the idol with its small stone base off the altar in his RIGHT hand, carefully, eyes fixed on it, and studies it for a moment. As he studies it, a slow devilish grin spreads across his face, one corner of the mouth first, then wide, eyes bright and narrowed: the private smile of a man declaring a monumental victory known only to himself, no one else in the room to see it. Fine dust begins to sift down through the light beam. The idol stays in his right hand from this moment on.
CUT 2, medium close-up, handheld, about 3 seconds: the floor begins to shake. The camera trembles. Dust and small stones fall from the ceiling and the vines sway. He looks up sharply toward the ceiling, alarmed, holding the idol tight in his right hand.

PERFORMANCE
Cut 1: careful hands, then the slow devilish grin of secret, monumental triumph, savouring the moment alone. Cut 2: the grin drops at once into sudden alarm: eyes widen, breath held, jaw tight. No speaking.

PHYSICS
The idol has real weight for its size and his wrist dips slightly as he lifts it. Dust drifts in the beam, then falls faster when the floor shakes. The cracks in the flagstones widen slightly. Vines sway.

LIGHTING
Cool teal-green daylight from the broken roof, soft fill, deep shadow in the corners. No warm light except the natural reflection on the gold.

AUDIO
Stone grinding rumble, falling dust and pebbles, distant jungle insects, one low heartbeat-like thump as he lifts the idol. No speech, no music.

STYLE
