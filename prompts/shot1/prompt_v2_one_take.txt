SCENE CONTEXT
Inside an ancient, overgrown jungle temple chamber. Cool teal-green daylight falls through a collapsed roof. A young explorer lifts a small polished-gold idol off a stone altar. The temple reacts: dust falls and the floor shakes. He looks up, alarmed. Eight seconds, ONE continuous take with no cuts, no dialogue.

LOCATION MAP
Interior of the ruined chamber from Image 3. A weathered stone altar stands in the centre of the cracked flagstone floor. A shaft of teal daylight from the broken roof falls toward the altar, with mist and floating dust in the beam. Vine-covered carved walls behind. The idol sits on top of the altar. The camera works from the entrance side, facing the altar.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Wide-medium shot. The explorer stands at the altar, front three-quarter view, both hands reaching toward the idol. The idol sits on the altar top. The light shaft is behind him.

FORMAT MODE
ONE continuous take, no cuts, no edits, no jump, no change of angle. A single unbroken camera move: it starts in a medium shot and pushes in slowly and steadily to a medium close-up. For the first part of the move the camera is smooth and steady. When the floor starts to shake, the same camera, without cutting, begins to tremble like a handheld camera and keeps pushing in.
FIRST PART, about the first 5 seconds, steady slow push-in: the explorer lifts the idol with its small stone base off the altar in his RIGHT hand, carefully, eyes fixed on it, and studies it for a moment. As he studies it, a slow devilish grin spreads across his face, one corner of the mouth first, then wide, eyes bright and narrowed, a brow lifted: the private smile of a man declaring a monumental victory known only to himself, no one else in the room to see it. Fine dust begins to sift down through the light beam. The idol stays in his right hand from this moment on.
SECOND PART, about the last 3 seconds, the same take, the camera now trembling: the floor begins to shake. Dust and small stones fall from the ceiling and the vines sway. His grin drops at once into sudden alarm and he looks up sharply toward the ceiling, holding the idol tight in his right hand.

PERFORMANCE
First part: careful hands, then the slow devilish grin of secret, monumental triumph, savouring the moment alone. Second part: the grin drops at once into sudden alarm: eyes widen, breath held, jaw tight. No speaking.

PHYSICS
The idol has real weight for its size and his wrist dips slightly as he lifts it. Dust drifts in the beam, then falls faster when the floor shakes. The cracks in the flagstones widen slightly. Vines sway.

LIGHTING
Cool teal-green daylight from the broken roof, soft fill, deep shadow in the corners. No warm light except the natural reflection on the gold.

AUDIO
Stone grinding rumble, falling dust and pebbles, distant jungle insects, one low heartbeat-like thump as he lifts the idol. No speech, no music.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action adventure thriller, 16:9 anamorphic look, fine film grain, muted green and teal palette.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The idol stays palm-sized, about as tall as his hand from wrist to fingertip and clearly smaller than his head, the same size relative to his hand in every frame, and identical in every frame: polished gold, egg-shaped head, round ear discs, spiral engraving, closed eyes, closed thin mouth, small stone base attached. It is in his right hand after he lifts it. No glow, no cracks and no light effects on the idol. The explorer's face matches Image 1 in every frame. His clothes are dry and dusty, not wet. Only the idol and the explorer move; the altar stays in place. There are no cuts, no jump and no change of angle at any point: one continuous camera move from the first frame to the last.
