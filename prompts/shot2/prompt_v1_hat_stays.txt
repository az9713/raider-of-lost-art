SCENE CONTEXT
Seconds after the temple began to shake. The young explorer, still dry, bolts out of the ruins onto a narrow, vine-covered stone ledge along a jungle cliff and sprints toward the edge of a huge waterfall, the small gold idol in his right hand. He does not slow down: he leaps off the end of the ledge into the roaring white water. Seven seconds, ONE continuous take with no cuts, no dialogue.

Dense green jungle wall on the left of the ledge, vines hanging. On the right, the white curtain of the waterfall drops into a misty green valley far below. Thick mist and spray in the air. The ruins entrance is behind the camera, to the left.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Wide shot from behind and slightly above. The explorer is already running away from the camera along the ledge, small in frame, toward the waterfall, idol in his right hand, satchel bouncing. The waterfall roars ahead.

FORMAT MODE
ONE continuous take, no cuts, no edits, no jump, no change of angle. A single unbroken camera move. First part, about 4 seconds: the camera tracks fast behind and slightly above him as he sprints along the ledge, steady with a slight handheld push. Second part, about 3 seconds, the same take: as he reaches the broken end of the ledge and leaps, the camera keeps moving, rises and pulls back and out into a wide shot, following his arc out over the drop toward the white curtain of water. The take ends as he drops into the white water and spray and disappears into it.

PERFORMANCE
Total commitment. He runs with his head down and arms pumping, the idol held tight in his right hand. At the last step his jaw is set, a flash of wild nerve, no hesitation. In the air his body stretches out. No speaking.

PHYSICS
Wet stone under his boots, small splashes of mist from each step, loose pebbles kicked off the ledge. His jacket and satchel whip behind him. He takes off from the very end of the ledge with one driving leg. His arc is a real jump, not a flight: he rises a little, then falls toward the water. Spray from the waterfall drifts across the frame. He stays DRY until the moment he hits the water.

LIGHTING
Cool teal-green overcast daylight, soft, no hard sun. Pale white water, deep green jungle. No warm light except the natural reflection on the gold.

AUDIO
Boots on wet stone, heavy breathing, the roar of the waterfall rising steadily, the whoosh of the leap, a sharp rush as he enters the water. No speech, no music.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action adventure thriller, 16:9 anamorphic look, fine film grain, muted green and teal palette. Same look and colour grade as the previous shot.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The idol stays palm-sized, polished gold with closed eyes and a closed thin mouth, small stone base attached, in his RIGHT hand in every frame until he enters the water. No glow, no cracks, no light effects on the idol. The explorer's face matches Image 1. His hair stays short bleached blonde in every frame, never black or brown. His clothes are dry until he enters the water. There are no cuts, no jump and no change of angle at any point: one continuous camera move from the first frame to the last. The ledge always runs left to right and ends at the waterfall on the right. He jumps toward the waterfall, never away from it.
